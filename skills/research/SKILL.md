# Research Skill

## Purpose
Produce evidence-led research, not unsupported summaries.

## Process
1. Clarify the research question and scope.
2. Break the question into answerable subtopics.
3. Search for primary and authoritative sources first.
4. Record title, publisher/organization, date when visible, URL and relevant evidence.
5. Compare multiple sources and identify disagreements.
6. Distinguish facts, estimates, opinions and assumptions.
7. State what could not be verified.

## Quality rules
- Never fabricate a source, quote, date or statistic.
- Prefer original studies, official datasets, laws, filings and institutional reports.
- Treat search snippets as discovery aids, not final evidence.
- Avoid claiming causation from correlation.
- Explain limitations and source recency.
